# 01 · Product and business plan

## Objective

Build a repeatable client acquisition system for a U.S.-focused agency offering web design, web development, Google Ads, Facebook/Instagram ads, social media management, SEO, conversion improvements, and related business marketing services.

Success means relevant conversations, qualified opportunities, signed clients, and profitable delivery. A large database is useful only when its records are accurate, usable, and connected to a suitable offer.

## Scope established by the owner

- Discover as many local businesses as practical across all 50 U.S. states and Washington, D.C. Keep territories as an optional geography expansion.
- Include businesses of every size and supported category. Do not exclude leads because their revenue is unknown or their expected budget is below $25,000/month.
- Group leads by service fit, outreach priority, and an evidence-based budget hypothesis.
- Support focused service sales around $5,000/month as well as larger engagements around $25,000/month or more. Smaller or one-time projects remain possible.
- Use ordinary automation for most work and selective AI for interpretation and writing.
- Include browser-based research where permitted, business email verification, customized outreach, proposal preparation, and human calling tasks.

## Core journey

1. An operator selects states, counties or metros, and industries.
2. The platform estimates source coverage, request volume, and cost before running.
3. Approved connectors import business records and preserve their origin.
4. The system resolves duplicates and identifies the business's own website.
5. Low-cost extraction finds business contact routes and service opportunity signals.
6. The dashboard places accounts into service and budget groups, with an unknown group for insufficient evidence.
7. A salesperson checks evidence, chooses a contact and offer, and reviews a draft.
8. Eligible outreach runs through an approved sending channel; every reply or opt-out stops the sequence.
9. Discovery confirms needs, authority, timing, and budget before a detailed proposal.
10. Won clients move into delivery; outcomes improve future prioritization.

## Commercial offers: starting hypotheses

These are our proposed packages, not market pricing claims or commitments to prospects. Final prices require delivery scope, staffing, and margin review.

| Offer | Suitable situation | Initial pricing direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website project | Missing, outdated, or difficult-to-use site | Scope-based one-time price; optional care plan |
| Focused service | One clear need such as SEO, ads management, or social content | Quote individually; around $5k/month where scope supports it |
| Combined growth | Website improvements plus selected acquisition channels | Approximately $5k–$10k/month |
| Regional growth | Several locations or channels and stronger reporting needs | Approximately $10k–$25k/month |
| Larger partnership | Complex multi-location growth with execution capacity | $25k+/month when justified |

Advertising spend is separate from agency service fees. One-time website fees, recurring fees, software subscriptions, and media spend must be separate proposal line items. An estimated budget bucket never becomes a quote automatically.

Lead with one relevant problem and one outcome to investigate. Keep the full service menu available for discovery and expansion. Avoid claiming “industry leading” results until we have evidence, case studies, and delivery capability to substantiate the claim.

## MVP and later scope

MVP: internal agency workspace, geography/category campaigns, approved-source imports, website extraction, deduplication, evidence, scoring, budget groups, verification, exports, draft review, basic email sequences, reply handling, suppression, manual calling tasks, and sales pipeline.

Later: more source connectors, more states and categories, optional CRM integration, consented SMS, reporting automation, measured budget calibration, referral tracking, and capacity-aware sales forecasting.

Full agency fulfillment software, autonomous closing, social network mass messaging, and a public data resale marketplace are outside the first release. They introduce different product requirements and should not delay lead acquisition.

## Product boundaries

We can maximize measured coverage; we cannot promise an exhaustive list of every operating business. Some businesses have no public footprint, sources overlap, licenses may belong to individuals, and records go stale.

Budget estimates are sales hypotheses. Acquisition interest is uncertain until engagement. Technical email verification is distinct from permission to contact and from inbox placement. Each of those concepts has a separate field and workflow.

## Business validation

Before expanding heavily, review 100 varied accounts and test whether the data identifies real service needs. After a permitted outreach pilot, inspect positive replies and qualified meetings by industry, offer, and estimated budget band. Keep small businesses in scope even when early larger accounts perform well.

For high-ticket sales, prepare credible work samples, delivery partners or staff, a discovery process, reporting examples, a contract, and a clear statement of work. The software supplies opportunities; the agency must supply proof and execution.
